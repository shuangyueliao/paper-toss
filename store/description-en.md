# Paper Toss — Store Submission Materials

## Short description (CrazyGames / GameDistribution / itch.io)

Flick crumpled paper into the office wastebasket. The bin moves and the
fan shifts the wind every round — read it, aim your swipe, and sink
streaks for bonus points. Endless or 60-second challenge.

## Details

- Genre: Arcade / Casual / Sports
- Controls: Swipe up (touch) or click-drag (mouse) to throw. Keyboard:
  Space / ↑ for a straight toss, M to mute.
- Features: Random bin spots (left/center/right, near/far), wind 0-3 with
  animated fan and wind sign, rim bounces that can still drop in, ball
  stays in the bin (fills up, empties at 10), streak bonuses +2/+5/+10,
  endless & 60-second modes, best score & longest streak saving, mute
  toggle, procedural graphics & sounds, single-file HTML5 (no external
  assets), works offline once loaded.
- Tech: Vanilla JavaScript, Canvas, WebAudio. No frameworks, no tracking.
