# Paper Toss

Free single-file HTML5 office arcade game. Swipe up to flick crumpled paper
into the wastebasket — random bin spots (left/center/right, near/far), a
desk fan that changes wind strength and direction every round, streak
bonuses at 3/5/8 in a row, endless or 60-second modes. One HTML file, zero
assets.

**Play:** https://shuangyueliao.github.io/paper-toss/

- Swipe / drag / Space to throw, procedural WebAudio sounds (paper swish,
  rim clank, basket rattle, fan ambience)
- Procedural office & crumpled-paper visuals, bin fills up and empties at 10
- Best score & longest streak saved locally, no dependencies
